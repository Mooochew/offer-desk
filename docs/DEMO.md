# Recording the demo (1–2 minutes)

The video counts for 30% of the grade. The plan: a silent screen recording of the live app, edited afterward with captions, sped-up waits and an original background track. It's exported as a landscape 1080p MP4 and posted to TikTok.

Live app: https://offer-desk-teal.vercel.app

## 1. Before you record
- [ ] Record on the day you submit. Live job numbers change daily. Don't run `check:ui`, `check:agent` or the evals that day; they use the same per-network budget.
- [ ] Use a fresh incognito window, full screen (F11), with zoom at 100% (Ctrl+0).
- [ ] Turn off notifications and close other tabs.
- [ ] Record at 1920×1080: **Win+Shift+R** (Snipping Tool) or OBS, whole screen.

## 2. Record: one take, no voice
After each result appears, hold still for about 2 seconds. Move the mouse slowly to the thing in bold.

| # | Do | Pause on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Let the page sit for 3 s | **Start here** guide |
| 2 | **Load demo profile**, then **Seed sample pipeline** | Pipeline columns |
| 3 | Click the first suggestion: *Find entry-level backend and full-stack roles in SoCal or remote posted this week* | **Funnel bars**, source chips |
| 4 | Open **Evidence** on the top card | Quotes |
| 5 | *Save the top 5 and give me an apply checklist* | File card |
| 6 | *Mark the first 5 as applied today. I have an interview with [company from your top 5] Thursday at 2* | `.ics` card |
| 7 | *Archive everything I haven't touched in 3 weeks* | **Confirm dialog** (3 s), then Confirm |
| 8 | *Show my funnel this month* | Funnel chart |
| 9 | Scroll the **Action log** and open one **Arguments** | JSON arguments |

If a step goes wrong, keep recording, redo it, and note roughly when it happened.

## 3. Edit (done by Claude with ffmpeg)
- Cut or speed up the waits (6–14 s per turn).
- Add a large caption per step, readable even letterboxed on a phone.
- Add title and end cards with the repo and live URLs.
- Mix in an original instrumental track at low volume (no copyright claims).
- Export a 1080p MP4 of 60–90 s for review.
